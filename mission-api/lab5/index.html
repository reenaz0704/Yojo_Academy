Domain Expiration Investigation

Scenario

You are performing a domain intelligence assessment for YOJO Solutions. As part of the investigation, you need to determine when the company's domain registration expires and understand the risks associated with an expired domain.

Your task: investigate the domain's registration information and submit the expiration date on the YOJO Academy mission page.

Target domainyojosolutions.comOfficial website: https://www.yojosolutions.com/

What is a domain expiration date?

A domain name is registered for a specific period. The expiration date is the date associated with the end of that registration period in the domain registry's records.

Why does it matter?

If a domain is not renewed, the registrant may lose the right to use it. Depending on the registrar and the domain extension, it may enter a grace or redemption period and can eventually become available for someone else to register. Possible consequences:

  • Website downtime or loss of control over the domain.
  • Email delivery disruption.
  • Impersonation or phishing if another party obtains the domain.
  • Risk to services and accounts tied to the domain, especially where email addresses use it.
  • Reputational and business-continuity damage.

Expiration dates and renewal deadlines are not always identical, and recovery periods vary by registrar and domain extension.

How to investigate

  1. Note the target domain above: yojosolutions.com.
  2. Open a WHOIS or RDAP lookup service (options below).
  3. Search for the domain and open the registration record.
  4. Find the expiry field. It may be labelled Registry Expiry Date, Expiration Date or similar. Do not confuse it with the creation or last-updated date.
  5. Read the date carefully. Records often show a timestamp such as 2030-01-31T12:00:00Z: the date is the part before the T.
  6. Return to the YOJO Academy mission page, enter the date (for example 2030-01-31) and press Submit Answer.

Lookup services

Option 2rdap.org redirects to the right registry's RDAP record (raw JSON: look for the expiration event)
Option 3Verisign RDAP the .com registry's own record
Command linewhois yojosolutions.com or curl -s https://rdap.org/domain/yojosolutions.com

If one service is slow or unavailable, try another. They read the same registration data.

Scope

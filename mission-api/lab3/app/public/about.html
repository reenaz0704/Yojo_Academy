About Yojo Coffee

A neighbourhood roaster with a simple idea: great coffee, treated with care.

Our story

Yojo Coffee began in 2014 as a single secondhand roaster in a Southeast Portland garage. Friends kept asking for bags, so we opened a small cafe on Alder Street.

Today a team of twelve roasts, brews and packs every order by hand. We still know the name of every farmer we buy from.

Why YOJO Solutions?

Who we are

YOJO Solutions is a cybersecurity-focused organization dedicated to delivering practical cybersecurity training, industry-oriented internship programs, cybersecurity awareness initiatives, and professional skill development.

We focus on bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world cybersecurity requirements through hands-on training, practical labs, industry tools, live projects, mentorship, assessments, and career preparation.

Our approach goes beyond theoretical knowledge. We help students, fresh graduates, working professionals, educational institutions, and organizations develop the technical skills, practical experience, and professional confidence required to navigate today's evolving cybersecurity landscape.

Our vision

To build a generation of cybersecurity professionals who are skilled, practical, ethical, and industry-ready.

Our mission

To make cybersecurity education practical, accessible, and career-oriented while helping individuals and institutions build stronger cybersecurity capabilities.

At YOJO Solutions, we follow a practical, outcome-driven approach:

Learn→Practice→Build→Investigate→Report→Get Career Ready

Our programs combine expert-led instruction, hands-on labs, industry tools, real-world scenarios, projects, assessments, and professional reporting so learners can demonstrate their cybersecurity capabilities, not simply complete a course.

FAQ
Quick answers

    
Do I need prior cyber security knowledge?
No. The onboarding questionnaire is puzzles and problem-solving, not cyber trivia. It places you in Offensive, Defensive or GRC and your mentor builds from there.

    
What does the free plan include?
Starter labs in the cyber range, streaks, the weekly league and intro lessons. No card needed.

    
How does the placement assurance work?
Learners who complete the programme and meet attendance and assessment criteria keep receiving interview calls, referrals and mock interviews until placed. Final terms are pending owner and legal review.

    
Can I study online?
Yes. Batches run online and in our Visakhapatnam classroom. Recorded lessons are available for every live class.
